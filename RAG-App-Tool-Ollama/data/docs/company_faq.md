# About the nZone

Welcome to nZone. In 2011 when New Life Christian Church purchased the 83,000 sq ft beer distribution warehouse to host Sunday Church service they had a dream to use the facility to serve the community. New Life converted the warehouse into a multifunctional space to serve as a hub for the surrounding community and foster connections through faith, family, fitness, and fun. We continue to promote physical, mental, and spiritual well- being to this day.

Our goal is to create a safe environment where everyone can experience Gods grace and love. We believe in fostering a community that embraces compassion and genuine connections. Through fitness, family programs, and shared experiences, we aim to reflect Gods love in all that we do. Whether you’re here for a workout, a youth program, or a community event, you’re part of something bigger.

# Adult Leagues information

The nZone proudly offers year-round adult sports leagues for both basketball and soccer, giving players of all skill levels the chance to compete, stay active, and have fun in a well-organized, community-driven environment. With separate leagues running each spring, summer, fall, and winter, there’s always a new season right around the corner, so you never have to wait long to get back in the game.

Whether you’re a seasoned athlete or just looking to stay in shape and meet new people, The nZone’s adult leagues provide a flexible and welcoming environment. Players can join as part of a full team or sign up as free agents, and our staff works hard to ensure that every participant is placed and that teams are well-matched for competitive play.

What sets The nZone apart is our commitment to value and quality. League fees are lower than most competitors, making it one of the most affordable options in the area without sacrificing quality. Games are held at our top-tier indoor facility, featuring professionally maintained courts and turf fields, reliable scheduling, and a clean, comfortable atmosphere for both players and spectators.

Each season includes multiple games, refereed matchups, and a chance to compete in playoffs and championship rounds, where winners take home bragging rights and prizes. It’s more than just a league; it’s a community of athletes who return season after season.

Join the nZone adult leagues today and experience the difference: affordable pricing, elite facilities, and nonstop action all year long.
